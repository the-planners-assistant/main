Tools for better spatial planning.

The Planner's Assistant is an open-source project exploring connected, evidence-led computational tools for planning places and their futures. Its initial focus is English Local Plan preparation, analysis, examination and monitoring.

Explore the repositoryFollow development
The project

Infrastructure, not another isolated tool.

The aim is to develop reusable, inspectable planning capabilities and connect information, spatial analysis, evidence, policy and changing real-world conditions. The technical architecture and individual capabilities remain under active investigation.

Planning information

Researching versioned, linked representations for plans, policies, sites and supporting evidence.

Computation and analysis

Exploring spatial analysis, alternative strategies, traceable reasoning and evaluation.

Open stewardship

Developing source code, documentation, reproducible methods and a transparent contribution workflow.

Current status

Foundation stage.

The public repository is being established. This website is an early project home, not a functioning planning application or a claim that the proposed capabilities have been implemented.
